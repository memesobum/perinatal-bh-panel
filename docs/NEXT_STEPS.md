# Next steps

*Plain-language summary: what later versions could add. v0.1 is a first, complete national table; these are planned improvements, not promises.*

1. **Travel-distance capacity.** Count listings and clinicians within 30 and 60 minutes of each county's population center, so border counties get credit for nearby services.
2. **Smoothed rates for small counties.** Report capacity per 1,000 women aged 15 to 44 alongside per 1,000 births, or shrink small-county ratios toward the state average, so tiny counties stop dominating the ranks.
3. **Perinatal-specific need.** Add state-level PRAMS postpartum depressive symptom estimates as a context column, and county maternal overdose deaths if CDC WONDER provisional data become usable at county level without suppression.
4. **Medicaid and maternity-care context.** Add Medicaid postpartum coverage status by state and March of Dimes maternity care desert class where licensing allows.
5. **Mental health perinatal programs.** Watch for SAMHSA adding a pregnant/postpartum field to mental health listings; add Postpartum Support International provider directory counts if terms permit reuse.
6. **CCBHCs and home visiting.** Add certified community behavioral health clinic sites and MIECHV home visiting coverage when open site lists exist.
7. **Puerto Rico and territories.**
8. **Annual refresh.** Re-run each year with the new AHRF, PLACES, SVI, NPPES, and FindTreatment.gov pulls; publish as a new version under the same Zenodo concept DOI.
